REFUND POLICY

1. Customers can request a refund within 30 days
   of delivery.

2. Products must be returned in acceptable condition.

3. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days
   after approval.

4. Shipping fees are non-refundable.

5. Customers cannot receive a refund twice
   for the same order.

6. The refund amount equals the product price.
